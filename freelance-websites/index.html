04 — Process

From first message to launch in four steps

  1. 1

    Discuss

    We talk through your goals, audience, content and budget, and agree a written scope and quote.

  2. 2

    Design

    I propose the page structure and visual direction, and we agree on it before building starts.

  3. 3

    Build

    I develop the site, then test it across phones, tablets and desktops and in major browsers.

  4. 4

    Launch

    I deploy the site, check every link and form, and hand everything over to you.

How revisions work

Each package includes the revision rounds listed in its description. A round is one consolidated list of changes to the agreed design and content. Extra rounds, or new features outside the agreed scope, are quoted before any extra work starts.

What you receive

A responsive, tested website deployed to your chosen host, all source files, and a short guide to editing text, images and contact details. Once the final balance is paid, the website files are yours.

07 — FAQ

Questions, answered

Something not covered here? Send me a message.

How much does a website cost?

Packages start at $40 for a single-page personal website and $100 for a business site of up to three pages. Larger or more complex projects are quoted individually. You’ll receive a written quote before any work begins.

How long does development take?

As a guide, a single-page website usually takes a few days once your content is ready, and a multi-page business site one to three weeks. We’ll agree a timeline for your specific project up front.

Will my website work on mobile?

Yes. Every website is built mobile-first and tested at phone, tablet and desktop widths before launch.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. I can rebuild an existing site with a modern, faster design, keeping the content that still works. Send me the link and I’ll suggest an approach.

Do you provide hosting?

I don’t sell hosting. I can deploy your site to a free static host such as GitHub Pages, Netlify or Vercel, or to a hosting account you own. Domain names and any paid hosting are paid for directly by you.

Can I update my website later?

Yes. The code is clean and documented, and you’ll get a short guide to editing text and images. If you’d rather I make changes, small updates can be quoted as needed.

What do you need before starting?

Your goals, the text you want on the site (or notes I can work from), your logo and photos if you have them, examples of websites you like, and access to your domain or hosting if you already have one.

How many revisions are included?

The Starter and Business packages include one revision round. Additional rounds can be added and are quoted before work starts.

Do you build online stores?

For small catalogues, a product showcase with WhatsApp or enquiry-based ordering works well and is far cheaper to run. A full online store with checkout and payments can be discussed and quoted separately.

How do payments work?

Payment terms and accepted payment methods are agreed in writing before work starts — usually a deposit to begin, with the balance due before final handover.
