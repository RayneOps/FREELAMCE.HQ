Privacy

Privacy notice

Short version: this website does not track you, and anything you send is used only to reply to you.

What this website collects

This is a static website. It does not use cookies, analytics, advertising pixels or any other tracking. The web host may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses and pages requested) for security and reliability, as most hosts do.

When you contact me

If you send an enquiry, it reaches me through the method you choose — your own email app, WhatsApp, or a form-handling service if one is configured on this site. I receive the details you enter (such as your name, email address and project description) and use them only to respond to your enquiry and, if we work together, to deliver your project.

I don’t sell or share your information, and I don’t add you to mailing lists.

How long I keep it

Enquiries that don’t lead to a project are deleted within 12 months. Project correspondence is kept for as long as needed to support the project and meet any legal obligations.

Your choices

You can ask me at any time to see, correct or delete the information you sent. Use the contact options on the home page.

Concept demos

The demonstration websites linked from this site feature fictional businesses. Their forms and buttons do not send or store any information.
