The studio

A small studio with big attention to detail.

We opened Ivory & Oak for people who want great hair without the rush. Our stylists specialise in precision cutting, colour that respects the health of your hair, and natural hair care for every texture.

Expect warm light, good coffee, honest advice and products we genuinely use ourselves.

  • Consultation firstEvery service begins with a short chat about your hair and routine.
  • Gentle on your hairLow-damage colour and nourishing treatments as standard.
