The problem

Your team’s week is scattered across five different tools.

Plans live in a doc, updates in chat, decisions in someone’s head. Small teams lose hours each week just figuring out what’s going on.

01

Status meetings that could be a message

Everyone stops working to say what they’re working on.

02

Priorities that drift by Wednesday

The Monday plan is forgotten as soon as new requests arrive.

03

Decisions nobody can find later

The “why” behind a choice disappears into a chat thread.

Features

Everything a small team needs. Nothing it doesn’t.

Tidewell is deliberately focused: one weekly rhythm, three simple views, zero setup.

Weekly plans

Agree on the week’s priorities on Monday, then track them on one board.

Async check-ins

Short written updates replace daily stand-ups. Read them when it suits you.

Decision log

Record what was decided, by whom and why — searchable forever.

Goals that connect

Link weekly tasks to quarterly goals so progress is always visible.

A calmer inbox

One digest per day instead of a notification for every change.

Works with your tools

Planned integrations with calendars, chat and code hosting.

How it works

A weekly rhythm in three steps

  1. 1

    Plan on Monday

    Each person adds their priorities. Tidewell suggests unfinished work from last week.

  2. 2

    Check in async

    A two-minute written update each day replaces the stand-up meeting.

  3. 3

    Review on Friday

    See what shipped, log decisions and carry over what’s left — in fifteen minutes.

FAQ

Questions before you start?

Can’t find an answer? Email the team.

Who is Tidewell for?

Teams of roughly 3–30 people who plan work in weekly cycles — product teams, agencies, startups and operations teams.

Does it replace our project management tool?

For many small teams, yes. Larger teams often use Tidewell for the weekly layer on top of their existing tracker.

How does the private beta work?

Request access with your work email. We invite teams in small groups and ask for feedback along the way.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time, and you can export your data whenever you like.

Where is our data stored?

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Business plans can request a data-processing agreement.
