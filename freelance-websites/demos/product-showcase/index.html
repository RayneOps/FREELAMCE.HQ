Our story

From our grandmother’s kitchen to your shelf.

Oyela began with a family recipe: shea butter, slowly whipped with oils by hand. We still make every batch in small quantities, so each jar is fresh when it reaches you.

We keep our ingredient lists short and readable, choose recyclable packaging wherever we can, and only make products we use ourselves.

FAQ

Good to know

Are your products suitable for sensitive skin?

Most of our products are fragrance-free or lightly scented. If you have sensitive skin, patch-test first and ask us about specific ingredients.

How long do the products last?

Unopened, most products keep for 12 months. Each label shows the batch date and a recommended use-by period after opening.

Where do you deliver?

Delivery areas and costs are confirmed on WhatsApp when you place your order.

Can I return a product?

Unopened products can be returned within 7 days. Message us and we’ll arrange it.

Do you offer wholesale?

Yes, for salons and boutiques. Send us a message with your business details.

Questions about your skin? Ask us.

Our team is happy to recommend a routine. Send us a message — we usually reply the same day.
