Less hype.
More shipping.

LATTICE brings together engineers, researchers, founders and policymakers for two days of practical talks about building AI products that work for real people.

Every session is designed around something you can take home: a technique, a case study, a framework or a working demo. Expect hands-on workshops, honest panels and plenty of time to meet the people building next to you.

Event highlights

  • 2days of talks, workshops and demos
  • 3tracks: Build, Research and Society
  • 8hands-on workshops with limited seats
  • 1evening demo night for local startups

FAQ

Who should attend?

Engineers, data scientists, product managers, founders, researchers and anyone responsible for decisions about AI in their organisation.

Do I need a technical background?

No. The Society track is designed for non-technical attendees, and every talk is labelled by level.

Will talks be recorded?

Main-stage talks will be livestreamed and recorded. Workshops are in person only.

Can I get a refund?

Tickets can be transferred to a colleague at any time, and refunded up to 30 days before the event.

How do I apply to speak?

The call for proposals opens in early 2027. Register interest to be notified.
